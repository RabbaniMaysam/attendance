Attendance: privacy

This page is a course tool. Students of a class mark themselves present during class and answer in-class questions.

Sign-in. The page uses Google sign-in only to confirm the email address of the person using it. It reads the email address and the verified status from the sign-in token, nothing else. It has no access to Google accounts, mail, files, or contacts.

Data kept. For each class: the roster the instructor uploads (first name, last name, email), each student's attendance marks with their time, the answers to in-class questions, and an activity log of sign-ins and actions with their time. The data is stored on Cloudflare and is visible only to the instructor. Students see only their own marks and answers.

Retention. The instructor deletes a class's data when the course ends.

Sharing. The data is not shared with anyone and is used only to administer the course.

Contact. Questions go to the course instructor.
